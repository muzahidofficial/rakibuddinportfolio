Human Capital Management & Development Professional

Kazi Rakibuddin Ahmed is a Bangladeshi-American human capital management and development professional with more than 35 years of experience and expertise.

He played a vital role in introducing modern HR practices in Bangladesh beginning in the late 1990s.

His experience across diverse industries and multicultural settings in Saudi Arabia, the United States, and Bangladesh is reinforced by a strong academic background.

In 2016, his original work in recruitment and selection was recognized through the patent known as Rakib’s Recruitment Rule — R3.
