About

Human capital expertise shaped by 35+ years of leadership, learning, and organizational transformation.

Kazi Rakibuddin Ahmed is the Managing Director and CEO of ABCDE Consultancy Ltd, a concern of the American Bangladesh Center for Development Excellence.

His experience spans human resources, learning and development, organizational development, performance management, compensation and benefits, corporate leadership, training, research, and academia.

He is highly regarded as a corporate personality, trainer, and researcher, with a professional journey across prominent local and multinational organizations.
